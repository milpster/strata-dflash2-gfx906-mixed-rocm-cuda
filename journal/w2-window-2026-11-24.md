### Why generation stopped at ~512 in both arms

With `max_tokens 1024` and thinking on, the server's answer-room rule fires:

- `reserve = min(max(ANSWER_RESERVE_MIN, 1024 // 4), 1024 // 2) = min(max(512, 256), 512) = 512`
  (`run/port0218-server/serve/server.py:5513`; the same statement is at
  `v0215/serve/server.py:6419` and `serve/server.py:6423` in the three trees — the constant
  `ANSWER_RESERVE_MIN = 512` is line 225);
- the thinking budget is then capped at `1024 − 512 = 512`, and each boot's own launcher log
  says so: `[strata] thinking limited to 512 of max_tokens 1024 so the answer has room`
  (`logs/W2-A-dec-r1-boot.log:16,91`, `logs/W2-B-dec-r1-boot.log:18,94`).

The reply stops at the thinking cap, **symmetrically in both arms** — a property of the
request shape, not of either arm, which is exactly why the leg cannot produce a TG rate. The
shape decision that follows was already measured: at `max_tokens 2048` the cap is
`2048 − 512 = 1,536` and generation reached 1,536 (`logs/W3p-B-pp-r2.engine.log:110,119`;
`records/W3p-B-pp-r2/B-pp239k-2048.json`, `max_tokens 2048`). Not re-litigated here.

## What this cannot support

1. **No engine attribution.** A and B differ in engine build *and* server tree; only W4 (2×2,
   veto-only) separates them. Until W4 runs, every delta belongs to the pair.
2. **No TG rate, for any decode number.** 513/545 (A) and 512/533 (B) generated tokens are
   below the 1,000-token floor; +11.0 % is the contract's decode metric on a capped reply.
3. **No n=2 claim on the short shapes.** A=2, B=1; `W2-B-sht-r1` is a discarded boot and its
   lone pp2k record is out of the sample.
4. **No "within 5 %", no "no difference", no "confirm".** 2v2 gives minimum exact two-sided
   p = 0.33 and 1-vs-2 is weaker; there is no interval at all, and a wide interval straddling
   the delta boundary is INDETERMINATE, never "within ±delta".
5. **No prefill claim at the contract's pre-registered 5 %** either: −4.7 % is a direction
   from two boots per arm.
6. **Nothing about quality, and nothing pooled** with W1/W1d/W1f/W3 (different corpora paths,
   different engine pins). W2's records are this window's baseline only.

## Correction 2026-11-25 — the "B is not parked" shorthand in item 7

Reading the two boot logs side by side shows **both** arms park and **both** drop a
conversation to the disk tier. Item 7's "B's reply 'is not parked'" is a different log line:
it concerns a **superseded reply** — `the reply this request replaces is not parked (68 tokens
past the turn checkpoint at 2052)` (`W2-B-sht-r2.engine.log:121`) — not the conversation
snapshot.

| step | A (`W2-A-sht-r1`) | B (`W2-B-sht-r2`) |
|---|---|---|
| parked, in order | 2,088 → 8,063 → 2,120 tokens | 2,088 → 8,063 → 2,120 tokens |
| drop, at the 512 MiB ceiling | `:121`, 294 MiB resident | `:128`, 409 MiB resident |
| re-read 99 tokens | `6,865 ms (14.4 tok/s)` `:124` | `3,382 ms (29.3 tok/s)` `:124` |

The cache configuration is identical in both boots (`--conversation-cache-mib 4096`,
`-min-tokens 2048`, `-park-gain 4096`, `-ram-mib 512`, `-disk-mib 24576`, `-disk-ram-mib 64`,
`-evict-page-cache`); B adds only `--conversation-spill-mib 0`. The ~3.5 s inter-turn
difference is therefore the **cost of the restore**, and the 2.1× between the arms is the
engine build's restore path, not a setting. Item 7's conclusion — that only W4 can move
attribution off "pair" — stands; only its mechanism sentence was wrong. Levers and
verification: `journal/optimisation-report-2026-11-25.md` §3.
