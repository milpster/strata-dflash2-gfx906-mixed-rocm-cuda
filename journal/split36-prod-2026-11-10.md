## Audit findings and review limits

- Static analysis explains why a stage with few assigned layers can exhaust its
  own eligible expert pairs before exhausting VRAM. The stage fill iterates only
  `st.profile` (`src/program/generate.cpp:9097-9100,9136-9138` in the source worktree).
  Split-36's later stage has 6144 possible layer/expert pairs, but its actual fill
  is the measured 4842; candidate count must not be reported as resident count.
- Parent-verified handshake precedence: engine default helper reserve is
  512 MiB (`src/program/generate.cpp:784`), sent at `:9477-9480`; helper assigns
  `g_reserve=req.reserve_mib` and `g_slots=req.slots`
  (`tools/mixed_gpu/sidecar/helper_main.cpp:1457-1458`). Its process-wide fill
  cap comes from `o.fill_mib` at `:1635`. This agrees with the live boot report;
  the launcher's reserve 300 MiB is not the effective fill reserve.
- Parent checked `cache_spill::pick_device` selects the last stage device
  (`include/strata/core/cache_spill.hpp:36`). Spill is still OFF. The child's
  additional concerns about duplicated sizing rules and broader flag effects
  remain static review findings, not measured regressions or permission to
  enable the feature.
- Parent checked the candidate-refusal predicate tests IDs outside `[0,vocab)`
  (`include/strata/core/dflash_refusal.hpp:37-44`). The earlier request's fallback
  disabled local drafting for that request. This does not establish a physical
  root cause or explain the entire user-reported TG delta.

- `STRATA_VERIFY_COMPLEMENT` residency checksums are not generated-output SHA
