# pp239k: 1,024 generated tokens in every window ("same infill as before plus 1024tg") — 2026-11-24

CPU only: no boot, no leg, no GPU, no `:8009` traffic, no process control, no push. Prod kept
serving. Every number below is from `port_ab_contract`/`port_ab_prepare`/`port_ab_legs` output,
from a file hash, or from arithmetic over the measured W1 anchor rates.

## The directive

Operator, verbatim: **"same infill as before plus 1024tg."** The 58,423-token prefill leg must ALSO
generate 1,024 tokens in the SAME request, so one judge line yields both the prefill rate and a TG
rate that clears the `TG_CLAIM_MIN_GENERATED` = 1,000 floor. Until now `pp239k` carried
`max_tokens {W1: 8, W1d: 8, W1f: 8, W3: 1024}`: the 8-token tails are why W1/W1d/W1f produced
prefill only, and why every W1 leg printed `TG=ctx`.

## The change

| Site | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `contract.LEGS["pp239k"]["max_tokens"]` | `{W1: 8, W1d: 8, W1f: 8, W3: 1024}` | `1024` (plain int, every window) |
| `contract.LEGS["pp239k"]["kind_by_window"]` | W1/W1d/W1f `prefill`, W3 `tg` | unchanged — W1/W1d/W1f stay ONE request |
| `contract.gen_tokens` docstring | "falls back through entry[W2]" | says a plain int applies as written |
| `TG_CLAIM_MIN_GENERATED` comment | "W1's 8-token tail is a prefill leg" | says every pp239k window now generates 1,024 |
| `prepare.leg_section` metric text | "read_tps from the single fresh request" | adds "plus gen_tps off the SAME judge line … the prefill claim metric stays read_tps" when `claims_tg` |
| `legs.check` TG floor | any sub-floor TG leg is a problem | a record whose OWN cap was under the floor **warns** (pre-amendment baseline); a 1,024-capped leg that stops short still FAILs |
| `legs` selftest fixtures | `max_tokens 8` hardcoded, "8-token tail is context" case | cap read from the contract; new cases: 1,024 leg is `tg`, 32-token tail is `ctx`, old 8-token record warns |
| other leg caps | `dec144k` 1024, `pp2k`/`pp8k` 32, `ttft3` 64 | untouched |

`port_ab_prepare.py --prepare` and `--verify` both succeed (`verify: OK`). The manifest diff is the
cap, the metric sentence, the three harness-script shas, `prepared_at`, `git_head_at_prepare`, and the
`--max-tokens` word inside `command_A`/`command_B` for W1/W1d/W1f. All 42 config pins keep their
path/sha256/socket/cache/engine_log untouched: `--max-tokens` is a driver argument, not a config key.

## Budget arithmetic (W1, contract 5,400-9,000 s for 4 claim boots)

Measured W1 prefill rates: 252.1-301.6 tok/s, so the 58,423-token prefill alone is 193.7-231.7 s.

| Decode rate | 1,024-token decode | Leg (prefill + decode) | Delta vs 8-token tail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22.1 tok/s (contract fast) | 46.3 s | 240.0-278.1 s | +46.0 s |
| 21.6 tok/s (contract slow) | 47.4 s | 241.1-279.2 s | +47.0 s |
| 15.5 tok/s (W1 A-r1 measured worst) | 66.1 s | 259.8-297.8 s | +65.5 s |
| 8.4 tok/s (W1 B-r0 measured worst) | 121.9 s | 315.6-353.7 s | +121.0 s |

At the ~250 tok/s the parent quoted plus 21.6 tok/s decode: 233.7 + 47.4 = **281.1 s** for the whole
leg. Against the contract's upper per-boot bound of 2,250 s that leaves ~1,900 s of headroom, so
**no budget number is inflated or needs to be**: the derived range (5,220-8,700 s) and the contract
range (5,400-9,000 s) both still hold. The delta is reported, not absorbed into the table.

## What this makes impossible or awkward

1. **`max_context` is not a constraint.** The arm configs carry `--max-context 262144`; 58,423 prompt
   + 1,024 generated = 59,447 tokens = 22.7 % of the window. No trivial-context limit is approached.
2. **The W1 TG numbers are a prefill-leg tail, and must be labelled as such.** W1's gen rate comes off
   a warm DFlash-drafted generation that starts at 58k context with a 4,096-MiB conversation-cache
   budget, not from `dec144k`'s cold-then-cached pair. It is a legitimate ≥1,000-token TG figure for
   THIS prompt and boot, but it is a different shape from W2/W3's decode and may not be pooled with
   them. The measurement is new; nothing on disk says whether the 58k-context decode holds 21.6 tok/s.
3. **The decode cache-hit gate does NOT gain data.** W1/W1d/W1f stay `kind=prefill` (one request), so
   `reused_second`/`decode_from_request` remain absent and the "request 2 was served from the cache"
   check still applies only to W3/W2/W4. A 1,024-token tail does not turn a prefill leg into the
   two-request cached re-run, and it must not: making W1 `tg` would double the boot's wall clock and
   collide with W3.
4. **W1's historical records now warn.** They were written under the 8-token cap, so `--check W1`
   prints a WARN per record (not a FAIL). The two real FAILs on W1 (`W1-A-pp-r1` restart + one
   GPU memory-access fault) are pre-existing boot facts, identical before the change.
5. **The 1,024-token tail costs one extra HTTP body and no extra prefill.** The driver sends one
   request either way, so the arms stay matched; the only cost is ~47-121 s of wall clock per leg, which
   the window budget already covers.

## Not done, deliberately

- No boot, no leg, no measurement: the fixer lane is CPU-only and prod was serving throughout.
- W3's shape untouched beyond sharing the int (its own cap was already 1,024, `kind: tg`).
- No other leg's cap touched (`dec144k` 1024, `pp2k`/`pp8k` 32, `ttft3` 64).
- `bench/ab-v0210/rpcprof/out/` reads only: `--prepare` wrote byte-identical corpus/template/config
  artifacts (checksummed before and after; the only changed files are the untracked
  `boots/*.evidence.json`, which `--check` rewrites with volatile page-cache counters).
